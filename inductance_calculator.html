Online Inductance Calculator

This free inductance calculator helps electronics engineers, designers and hobbyists calculate the number of turns, AL value and inductance of a magnetic core. It supports nH, µH and mH inductance units.

The main relationship is L = AL × N², where L is inductance, AL is the core AL value and N is the number of turns.
